New Mexico Handyman Operations & Compliance

High Desert Handyman Services operates in full alignment with New Mexico handyman scope regulations. Services are focused on minor maintenance, small home repairs, non-structural framing modifications, light commercial assembly/fixtures, wood trim, tile repair, and subfloor patching (under the state general contractor threshold). For full major construction, structural builds, or licensed trade-restricted work (heavy electrical/plumbing), referrals are available upon request.
