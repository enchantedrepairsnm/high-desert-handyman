Deep-Dive Process: Bathroom Water Damage Recovery

STEP 1

Initial Inspection

Severe water rotting under worn tile flooring requiring complete tear-out.

STEP 2

Structural Subfloor

Cutting away rotted timber, reinforcing floor joists, and setting new subfloor plywood.

STEP 3

Sealing & Membrane

Commercial grade tanking and waterproof seal applied to protect against future moisture leaks.

STEP 4

Final Finish

Precision laid tile, sealed grout, and re-installed fixtures ready for daily family use.
