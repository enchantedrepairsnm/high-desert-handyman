Handyman Solutions Tailored to High Desert Homes

Hi, I'm Justin, owner and operator of High Desert Handyman Services. Living in the East Mountains means our homes take on unique weather, mountain settling, and maintenance challenges.

I specialize in providing high-quality finish work, custom floating shelving, tile backsplashes, deck/stair restorations, and light commercial upkeep with clear communication and honest pricing.

New Mexico Unlicensed Handyman Guidelines

Operating strictly within New Mexico state guidelines for unlicensed handymen (small repairs, installations, and maintenance projects under the state threshold limit of $7,200). For heavy structural re-framing or major trade permits, I am happy to refer licensed specialists!
